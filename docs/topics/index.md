# Topics

*Start from the job you want to do. Each guide is one page in four parts, so you can stop at the part you need.*

## What is here

Topics holds three kinds of page, all reached from the menu on the left:

| Group | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Step-by-step guides** | one job each (SSH, HTTPS, passwords...), in four parts, below |
| **[Algorithm reference](../algorithms/index.md)** | one page per algorithm, grouped by what it does, and the [full table](../algorithms/table.md) |
| **[Protocols, formats and tools](../protocols/index.md)** | TLS, SSH, Signal and the rest; key and certificate formats; the command-line tools |

The longer reading, the [book](../book/index.md) and the [history of secret writing](../books/history/index.md), is under **Books**.

## How each guide is laid out

| Part | Level | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| **In one minute** | Beginner | what it is for, in plain words |
| **Do it** | Intermediate | the commands, copy and run |
| **Expert** | Expert | every algorithm and its standard, the implementations, common mistakes, best practices |
| **Now and next** | Expert: the latest research | what changed lately and what is coming, with the date it was checked |

Each guide stays short and links to the [book](../book/index.md) for the why and to the [Algorithm reference](../algorithms/index.md) pages for the details.
