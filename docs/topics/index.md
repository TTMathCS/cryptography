# Topics

*Start from the job you want to do. Each guide is one page in four parts, so you can stop at the part you need.*

| Part | For whom | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| **In one minute** | anyone, school age up | what it is for, in plain words |
| **Do it** | anyone at a keyboard | the commands, copy and run |
| **Expert** | professionals, university | every algorithm and its standard, the implementations, common mistakes, best practices |
| **Now and next** | research | what changed lately and what is coming, with the date it was checked |

Each guide stays short and links to the [Book](../book/index.md) for the why and to the [Algorithms](../algorithms/index.md) pages for the details.
